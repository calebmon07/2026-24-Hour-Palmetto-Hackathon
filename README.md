This Repository's purpose is to display our thought process and our development of a functional medical chatbot with the help of API and AI dependencies. 

**Project**: MediBot - A friendly medical chatbot to help diagnose and guide users to take action

**What is the purpose of our Medical Chatbot?**
The purpose of this chatbot is not to replace medical professionals or the knowledge they posses, but to act as an alternative information source regarding the 
conditions of our users. 

**Why did we choose this project for our hackathon?**
When medical knowledge is needed immediately, many take to the web to manually search based on their symptoms, but guidance is often limited
as not all of a person's symptoms can be taken into account. With MediBot, you can accessibly enter all of your symptoms, and MediBot will
ask many important questions to diagnose your condition and given specific guidance and information necessary to you.
